Why five

Because the whole argument of this product is in the difference between them. The same house is worth a different maximum under every exit, and the gap between the best exit and the obvious one is usually the entire margin. A calculator that answers one of these questions will confidently give you the wrong number on four of these five properties — not because its arithmetic is wrong, but because it was answering a question you had not established was the right one.
